Initial Hypotheses:
  H0 (Null Hypothesis): μ1 = μ2 = μ3
  H1 (Alternative Hypothesis): At least one group mean is significantly different.

=========================================================================

=== Step 1: Normality Test (Shapiro-Wilk) ===
Group 1 - P-value: 0.2051, Mean: 5.30, Std Dev: 2.83
Group 2 - P-value: 0.2405, Mean: 11.80, Std Dev: 3.61
Group 3 - P-value: 0.2937, Mean: 15.60, Std Dev: 5.89

---> Conclusion: 
-----> All groups appear to be normally distributed(the p-values > 0.05) 
-----> Fail to reject H0

=== Step 2: Homogeneity of Variance Test (Levene's) ===
Levene's Test p-value: 0.4297

---> Conclusion: 
-----> Variances are homogeneous (p-value > 0.05). 
-----> Fail to reject H0.

=== Step 3: One-Way ANOVA Result ===
F-statistic : 14.5889
p-value     : 0.000051

---> Conclusion: 
-----> Reject H0. 
-----> At least one group mean is significantly different from the others.
